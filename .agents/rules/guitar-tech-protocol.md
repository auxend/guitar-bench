# Guitar Tech & Luthier Protocol

When discussing guitar setups, maintenance, troubleshooting, fretwork, or hardware modifications (especially for Fender offsets, Strats, and Teles), adhere strictly to the following luthier physics and diagnostic protocols:

## 1. Physical Axioms of Guitar Setup
- **The "Zero Fret" Nut Principle:** Nut slots only dictate clearance on open strings and the first 3 frets. The second any fret is depressed, the nut is removed from the vibrational circuit. Lowering nut slots will NEVER cause buzzing at higher frets (7–21).
- **The Offset Break Angle Paradox:** 
  - Never recommend slamming the bridge on a 24" short-scale offset (Jaguar/Mustang). Lowering the bridge flattens the break angle, reducing downward saddle pressure and causing violent bridge rattle and string dislocation.
  - Fix offset buzz by increasing break angle: install a **0.5°–1.0° neck pocket shim**, elevate the bridge, and run heavier strings (minimum 10.5 or 11s).
- **Relief Controls the Center, Bridge Controls the Register:**
  - Forward bow (excess relief) creates stiff action in frets 1–5 and high action at frets 7–12.
  - An ultra-low "slammed" setup requires a **near-flat neck (0.004"–0.006" relief)** combined with precision-leveled frets with upper-register fall-away.

## 2. Mandatory Order of Operations: T-R-A-I-N
Never diagnose or adjust out of sequence:
1. **Tune to Pitch:** Baseline string tension is mandatory.
2. **T: Truss Rod (Relief):** Measure gap at 8th fret with 1st fret capo'd and 17th fret held.
3. **R: Radius & Bridge Action:** Adjust overall bridge height and arc saddles to match fretboard radius.
4. **A: Action at Nut:** Measure open string clearance at 1st fret (or depress 3rd fret and check 1st fret whisper gap).
5. **I & N: Intonation & Testing:** Fine-tune string length at saddles and test whole-step bends in the upper register.

## 3. Diagnostic Rapid-Tree
- **Buzz on open strings only:** Nut slot too deep or back-angle cut improperly.
- **Buzz on frets 1–5:** Insufficient relief (back-bowed neck). Loosen truss rod counter-clockwise.
- **Buzz on frets 7–12:** Bridge action too low or excess neck relief forcing middle string interference.
- **Buzz/Choke on frets 14–21 (especially on bends):** Fretboard radius too round (7.25") or lack of upper-register fall-away.
- **Bridge rattles or grubs loose on offset:** Insufficient break angle (needs neck shim & bridge raise) or needs Blue Loctite 242.
